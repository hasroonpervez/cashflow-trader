# Verdict

The app is a well-engineered Streamlit desk with genuinely correct plumbing, atomic JSON writes, a shared curl_cffi session, thread contexts attached properly, disciplined `safe_html` escaping, and a handful of textbook-exact formulas (Black-Scholes price, gamma, theta, charm, Wilson lower bound), wrapped around a scoring layer whose numbers cannot be trusted. Roughly a dozen documented gates are provably dead (`squeeze` at `modules/options.py:1709`, the Pink-Diamond weekly filter at `options.py:983`, the `whale_bonus=2` tier at `options.py:909`, the IV-Rank gate at `renderers.py:1030`, the VIX macro gate at `sentiment_radar.py:710`), several displayed trading numbers are outright wrong (vanna sign at `options.py:108`, tape pillar at `signal_desk.py:587`, journal realized P&L at `config.py:161`), and the two headline win-rate statistics are contaminated by a full-sample lookahead at `options.py:829`.

**The single biggest risk is the sizing-to-alert path.** `scan_single_ticker` derives `Adj. Kelly %` from a hypothetical *short cash-secured put* (`options.py:1480-1493`), `continuous_kelly` clips at 100% *before* multiplying by `pop_mult` so the number can print **108.5% of bankroll** (`options.py:237`), and `renderers.py:2578-2591` converts that figure into a suggested **long share count** inside a Discord "💎 CONVICTION" buy alert, an alert that re-fires on every Streamlit rerun with no idempotency key (`renderers.py:2540`). A user can receive dozens of duplicate pings telling them to put more than their whole account into one name, sized by a trade they are not making.

**The "10x" branding is not currently honest.** `score_10x_potential` (`options.py:1168`) contains no payoff term, no probability term, no skew, no tail, and no headroom, a binary `mcap < $10B` flag is its entire upside-magnitude model. `radar_broad_filter` awards 55 of 100 points for having already gone up (`data.py:1015-1042`). The options layer is 100% short-vol, there is no long-call or debit-spread construction anywhere in the codebase, so the recommended execution *caps* the very asymmetry the labels advertise. And the repo's own `RESEARCH_UPGRADE.md:14` records Blue confluence as an entry being "regime beta ... NEGATIVE second half," while that exact signal is the app's loudest buy trigger and the module written to correct it (`modules/validated_signals.py`) is imported by nothing but its tests.

Two of the criticals below (Alpha Vantage adjustment basis, fabricated macro defaults) already have uncommitted fixes in the working tree, good work, commit it.

### 1. Short-put Kelly sizes long-equity buy alerts and can exceed 100% of capital, `modules/options.py:1504`, `modules/options.py:237`, `modules/renderers.py:2578`

`scan_single_ticker` builds `K_scan = price * 0.97`, `prem_scan = bs_price(..., 'put') * 0.85`, and a short-put Monte Carlo PoP (`options.py:1480-1493`), feeds them into `kelly_criterion(..., use_quant=True)`, and stores the result on every scanner row as `"Adj. Kelly %"` (`options.py:1653`). `renderers.py:2578-2579` then computes `_sugg = int((_eq_cap * (_kelly/100.0)) / _px)` and posts `Suggested shares: {_sugg}` in the CONVICTION Discord alert. A short-vol, capped-upside, fat-left-tail position's Kelly fraction is being used to size an uncapped long. Compounding it, `continuous_kelly` at `options.py:237` reads `max(0.0, min(1.0, allocation)) * 100 * correlation_haircut * pm`, the clip lands *before* `pop_mult`, so (a) whenever `f* = (μ−r)/σ² ≥ 2` the full-Kelly and half-Kelly branches return the identical value and the half-Kelly safety margin silently vanishes for exactly the high-drift names being hunted, and (b) `pop_mult = (avg_mc_pop/85)**0.5` pushes the output to **108.5%** at PoP 100. It is not even bounded there: `Opt.calc_kelly_haircut` returns 1.20 for a "true hedge" (`options.py:2381-2394`).

**Fix:** apply the haircut and `pop_mult` *inside* the clip and cap hard, `alloc = f_star * (0.5 if half else 1.0) * haircut * pm; return 100.0 * min(max(alloc, 0.0), 0.25)`. Separate the instruments: never surface a short-premium Kelly on a long-equity row. For skewed payoffs, replace Merton-Gaussian entirely with a discrete outcome solve (see Roadmap §4).

### 5. Vanna sign is inverted and rendered directly, `modules/options.py:108`

`vanna = phi_d1 * d2 / sigma * 0.01` is missing the leading minus. Since ∂d1/∂σ = −d2/σ, vanna = ∂Δ/∂σ = −φ(d1)·d2/σ. I re-derived it against the file: at S=100, K=120, T=1.0, r=0, σ=0.20 the code returns −0.014516 while a central finite difference of the code's *own* delta over σ±0.01 gives +0.014520, flipped at every moneyness (ATM, ITM, OTM, calls and puts). `charm` on line 112 correctly carries its minus, so this is an isolated slip. The value is printed verbatim at `renderers.py:1746` as "Vanna: … (Δδ per 1% IV)", telling a covered-call writer that an IV spike *reduces* their short-call delta when it increases it.

**Fix:** `vanna = -phi_d1 * d2 / sigma * 0.01`, plus a finite-difference regression test (see §14).

### 27. Both Hurst estimators are indistinguishable from noise at their thresholds, `modules/ta.py:455`

I re-implemented both exactly as written and ran 400 pure Gaussian random walks of 251 log returns. `calculate_hurst_exponent` (single-window log(R/S)/log(n), carrying the Anis-Lloyd small-sample bias): mean 0.524, sd 0.042 → **26.2% of pure random walks classified trending** (H > 0.55). `TA.hurst` (aggregated-variance slope/2, only 5 usable lags at n=251): mean 0.472, sd 0.105 → 24.2% trending and 39.8% mean-reverting, **64% of pure noise gets a non-random-walk verdict**. The two disagree by ~0.05 in mean on identical data while sharing the same 0.55/0.45 cutoffs. `_hurst_adaptive_signal_periods` (`options.py:776-789`) maps that coin flip to RSI 21/14/8 and rescales all three MACD periods for `detect_diamonds`, and `score_10x_potential` pays +1 for `hurst_trend` (`options.py:1211-1217`). The docstring at `ta.py:461-464` claiming SE ≈ 0.09 is wrong in both directions.

**Fix:** pick **one** estimator; apply the Anis-Lloyd correction (subtract E[R/S] under i.i.d. noise before the log ratio) or use a multi-window log-log regression; widen the band to ±2 SE of the measured null (≈0.42/0.58 for R/S, the variance-ratio estimator is too noisy to use at n=252 at all). Test: 200 seeded random walks, assert false-classification < 10%.

# The 10x Asymmetry Roadmap

The app currently ranks on unitless point-sums dominated by trend persistence. To make it an asymmetric-opportunity scanner, every ranking surface needs to output **money × probability**, and the execution layer needs an instrument that can actually express convexity. Ordered by dependency, each stage unlocks the next.

### Stage 0: Stop the bleeding (prerequisite, days)

Nothing below is meaningful while the ranking inputs are corrupted. Ship defects #1, #4, #10, #11, #12, #13, #16, #26, #27 first. Specifically: gate #11 must fail **closed**, #13's unknown-GEX must score 0, #16 must exclude the partial bar, and #4's lookahead must be removed before any statistic is measured. Then add the finite-difference greek test family (#14) and the real-shape panel fixture (#15) so the next regression is caught by CI rather than by a reader.

### Stage 1: Wire the validated-signals discipline into the live path

**Lands in:** `modules/validated_signals.py` (already written, imported by nothing), consumed by `modules/renderers.py:2536` and the watchlist ranker.

The repo already contains the correct epistemology and does not use it. Replace the CONVICTION trigger with `swing_pullback_signal(df_daily)`, the SMA20-reclaim close that actually passed the gate, and use `blue_diamond_rank(df_daily)["blue_score"]` to **order** the watchlist rather than fire alerts. Then make the gate structural: no score, flag or column may contribute points to any ranking until `promotion_gate(returns)` returns `pass=True`, and the UI must print `(n, CI_low, CI_high, h1_mean, h2_mean)` beside every score so an unvalidated heuristic cannot masquerade as an edge. Upgrade `bootstrap_ci` to BCa (`scipy.stats.bootstrap(..., method='BCa')`) since the 3R-target payoff is strongly right-skewed and the lower tail is the only one the gate reads, and add an `n_configs` parameter applying a Deflated-Sharpe or Bonferroni adjustment to alpha, `RESEARCH_UPGRADE.md:20` records 12 configs tested.

**Data needed:** none new. This is pure wiring plus one statistics upgrade.

### Stage 2: Build the point-in-time outcome ledger

**Lands in:** `modules/config.py` (`radar_add_hit` schema), a new `modules/outcomes.py`, rendered in `render_radar_tab`.

Everything downstream: base rates, EV probabilities, calibration, sizing, requires knowing what happened after a signal fired. Extend the persisted hit record with `entry_date`, `entry_price` (both already present), then add a backfill job that on each app load batches one `yf.download` over all open hits and writes `fwd_5d, fwd_21d, fwd_63d, fwd_252d, max_fwd_252d, min_fwd_252d`. Critically, **store the full Tier-1 output, not just the top 10**: recall is uncomputable without the names you *didn't* flag. Store the universe snapshot alongside (Stage 6) so replays use the universe as it stood.

Then render a calibration panel: hits bucketed by `explosion_score` decile against median forward return, hit rate for `max_fwd_252d >= +100%` and `>= +900%`, and a precision-recall curve. Publish the unconditional base rate next to every threshold, "this universe produced N names ≥ +100% in the last 12 months; the screen flagged M of them." Until those numbers exist, "🔥 HIGH CONVICTION" (`renderers.py:3138`) should read "unvalidated."

**Formula:** `precision@k = |flagged ∩ winners| / k`; `lift = precision@k / base_rate`.

### Stage 3: Replace point-sums with expected value as the ranking key

**Lands in:** `modules/options.py` (`compute_explosion_score` → `compute_expected_value`), consumed by `renderers.py:3101`, `data.py` results sort, `renderers.py:2608`, `sentiment_radar.py:673`.

`calc_ev` exists (`options.py:134-138`) and is used in exactly two short-premium places. Make EV the ranking key everywhere. Per candidate define a discrete outcome set with an explicit, auditable probability vector:

```
EV = Σ pᵢ · rᵢ        rank on EV;  secondary key EV / P(total loss)
```

Two independent probability sources, blended:
1. **Risk-neutral, from the chain**: `P(S_T > K) ≈ -∂C/∂K` by finite difference across adjacent strikes on a liquidity-filtered chain (OI ≥ 100, spread/mid ≤ 0.25). This is free and already fetched.
2. **Historical base rate, from Stage 2**: `P(max_fwd_252d ≥ x)` conditioned on the candidate's decile in each screen axis.

Display the three inputs (upside %, probability, downside %) beside the number so the assumption is auditable, and show the payoff ratio `max_gain / max_loss` explicitly. A 10x screen should **refuse to rank** anything whose modelled payoff ratio is below ~5:1.

### Stage 4: Sizing for skewed payoffs

**Lands in:** `modules/options.py:218` (`continuous_kelly`), `modules/renderers.py:2578` (the alert path).

Merton `f* = (μ−r)/σ²` is derived for a levered position in the underlying under Gaussian returns. For a thesis whose modal outcome is −100%, applying a Gaussian-variance Kelly to an *estimated in-sample drift* is precisely the estimator that blows up. Replace it for convex positions:

```
f* = argmax_f  Σᵢ pᵢ · ln(1 + f·rᵢ)        (numeric, over the Stage-3 outcome set)
deploy = min(0.25 · f*, per_name_cap)
```

with `per_name_cap` an absolute 0.5-2% of equity per lottery ticket, since −100% is genuinely on the table. Keep `continuous_kelly` only as an upper bound for equity/pyramid sizing, fix the clip ordering (#1), and **label the instrument** on every sizing field, a short-put Kelly and a long-share count must never share a column. Discrete binary Kelly (already parameterised at `options.py:1499-1503` and thrown away) is the right primary for defined-risk option positions because it matches the payoff.

### Stage 5: Convexity screens: coil, cheap optionality, catalyst window

**Lands in:** `modules/data.py` (`radar_broad_filter` re-weighting), `modules/options.py` (`evaluate_asymmetric_convexity_sieve` rebuild), a new IV-history store.

**5a. Separate the coil axis from the trend axis.** Today 55 of 100 Tier-1 points require the stock to have already gone up (`data.py:1015-1042`). Rank on the coil; use trend only as a pass/fail directional filter worth zero points:

coil_z      = -z(BBW percentile over 252d)
duration    = consecutive sessions in the bottom BBW decile
contraction = ATR(5) / ATR(50)
accumulation= OBV slope while |price slope| ≈ 0

```
Then reconcile with `sentiment_radar.attention_stage`, the two radars currently implement opposite theories of earliness (`STAGE_LATE_ROC = 0.15` labels a mover "you're late" while Tier-1 pays it 55 points). Adopt one shared earliness definition and assert it in a test.

**5b. Build a real IV percentile.** `compute_iv_rank_proxy` is a term-structure cross-section, not a 52-week rank, and its one broken caller returns 50 forever (#12). Persist a daily row of `(date, ticker, CM30_ATM_IV)`, 30-day constant-maturity ATM IV interpolated from the two bracketing expiries, to local parquet on every scan, then `iv_pctile = rank of today's CM30 within the trailing 252 stored observations`, **showing n** so a 20-day history is never presented as a yearly rank. This series is the precondition for cheap-optionality screening.

**5c. Replace the strike-matched skew with a delta-matched risk reversal.** `calc_vol_skew` takes the single nearest strike to ±10% from one arbitrarily-chosen expiry on raw Yahoo IV with no liquidity filter (`options.py:245-259`, `1381`), one stale quote sets `skew_ratio = 3.0`. Interpolate IV at delta = ±0.25 from a fitted smile across two expiries on an OI/spread-filtered chain, and return `None` rather than passing when the filter empties the chain.

**5d. Convert the AND-conjunction sieve into a scored screen with coverage reporting.** Return `{'score': weighted_sum, 'n_evaluable': k, 'n_missing': m, 'gates': {name: 'pass'|'fail'|'no-data'}}` so a data gap never renders as a verdict. Calibrate each threshold against the Stage-2 label `max_fwd_252d >= +100%`, plot hit rate by float decile, short-interest decile, BBW decile, and set cutoffs where lift actually appears rather than at round numbers.

**5e. Add a catalyst window**: the axis the app has none of. Days-to-earnings already exists in `data.py`; add FDA PDUFA dates, court dates, lockup expiries, index-inclusion decisions where obtainable. Require any long-convexity structure's expiry to sit **beyond** the dated catalyst.

**Data needed:** FINRA bi-monthly short-interest file (free, dated, replaces unreliable yfinance `info`), a listings file for the universe, and the locally-persisted CM30 IV series.

### Stage 6: Give the app an instrument that can express asymmetry

**Lands in:** a new `Opt.long_convexity_candidates` beside `cash_secured_puts` (`options.py:2218`), rendered as its own ranked table.

rank on         EV / cost               (Stage 3)
```

Refuse to surface anything with `payoff_ratio < 5`. Label the instrument on every row so a short-vol Kelly can never again size a long position.
